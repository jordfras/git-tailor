One thing to know. While the detail view is open, the operation keys do nothing
at all: it is a place for reading.

h says as much. The same key that listed every operation in the commit list
lists none of them here: searching, context, and ways to move around.

[0.9]
Close the view with q, and that same key does what it always did.

[0.9]
Which is the whole of the detail view: open it on anything you are about to
rewrite, and close it before you rewrite it.
