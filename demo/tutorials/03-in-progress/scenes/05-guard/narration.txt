By default a rewrite refuses to run at all while you have uncommitted changes.

[0.9]
Start it with dash dash autostash and git-tailor puts them aside first and
brings them back afterwards, staged and unstaged exactly as they were.

[0.8]
Or set G T autostash in your environment, once, if that is how you always want
to work. It is not the default because stashing and reapplying leaves entries
in your ref-log that you never asked for.
