Every operation is undoable. Not just the last one: back through the whole
session, one step at a time.

[0.9]
Redo walks the other way, the same distance.

[0.8]
And the history outlives the program. Quit, reopen git-tailor tomorrow, and the
undo stack is still there. It is kept in a journal file under dot git, so
nothing depends on git-tailor still running.

[0.9]
It holds while the branch is where git-tailor left it. Move it yourself — in
another shell, even with git-tailor still open — and the stack is dropped rather
than replayed onto something it no longer fits.
