Below the commits are two rows that are not commits: what you have staged, and
what you have not. They sit at the bottom because that is where they belong in
the order: newer than anything committed.

[0.9]
They sit in the matrix like everything else, so a column tells you which commit
your uncommitted work belongs with, before you decide what to do with it.

[0.9]
eigh stages every tracked change. Shift eigh unstages. c commits what is staged.
