Dim means the same thing wherever you see it: context, not about the commit you
have selected.

[0.7]
In the matrix that is a column your commit does not touch. In the list, a dim
green commit can still fold into an earlier one; it just cannot fold into the
one you are looking at. The color keeps its meaning, and the dimming says it is
not about you.

[0.9]
So before you reorder anything, or squash anything, you can see which commits
are entangled and which are not. The matrix is not decoration: it is the answer
to "is this safe", available before you try it.
