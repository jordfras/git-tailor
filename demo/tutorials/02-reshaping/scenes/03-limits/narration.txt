Two things to know before you rely on it.

[0.8]
The grouping is not a property of the commit. It comes from the whole branch:
which regions each commit touched. Start git-tailor from a different base and
the same commit can split differently.

[0.9]
And when everything a commit changed belongs to one group, there is nothing to
separate. git-tailor says so, rather than making a split that means nothing.
