One thing before you run this on a branch of your own.

[0.8]
What you are looking at is your branch, back to the point where it left the
default branch. git-tailor works that out on its own.

[0.9]
But if you cut this branch from another one, and that is where it goes back to,
name that branch instead. The list becomes what you added on top of it, without
the commits you inherited.

[0.9]
Or dash dash all, for the whole history, down to the first commit.

[0.8]
The next tutorial does the operations: splitting a commit, folding one into
another, moving them around.
